# Social & blog content

## Templates (match structure and voice)

- LinkedIn posts: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/templates/linkedin-posts/` (template1–4.md — pick the format that fits the goal).
- Blog articles: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/templates/blogs/` (e.g. `whats-broken-in-playable-ads.md`).

## Optional grounding

- For persuasion, review `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/frameworks/bias_checklist_munger.md`.
- For problem-centric framing, review `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/frameworks/gap_selling.md`.

## Process

1. Confirm the goal, audience, and platform.
2. Pick the matching template.
3. Generate at least 3 variations; critique each on hook strength, clarity, and value; select the best.
4. Present the winner plus a one-line note on why.

Follow the overall [vibewriting-method] process.
