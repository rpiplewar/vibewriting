# Email communication

## Reference documents (read before drafting)

1. Primary methodology: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/frameworks/gap_selling.md` — problem-centric communication.
2. Psychological principles: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/frameworks/bias_checklist_munger.md` — cognitive biases to activate.

## Process

1. Analyze the provided customer context.
2. Review the reference documents for applicable principles.
3. Generate **at least 3 variations** of the email.
4. Critique each variation against the frameworks.
5. Select the best version against the user's objective.

## Quality gates

- Alignment with the Gap Selling methodology (problem-centric focus).
- Appropriate cognitive biases activated.
- Value-first, not "salesy."

## Output

1. Proposed email content.
2. Brief explanation of the principles applied.
3. Rationale for the chosen approach.

Do not proceed without reviewing the reference documents. Follow the overall [vibewriting-method] process.
